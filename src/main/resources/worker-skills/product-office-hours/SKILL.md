# Product Office Hours

Ask for a concrete user, their current workaround, the trigger and the consequence of doing nothing.

Separate observed demand from assumptions.

Compare the smallest useful version with plausible alternatives and agree on a measure of success.

Return the narrowed scope, open decisions and the next thing to learn.

Do not start implementation during discovery.

Method adapted for AIW from [upstream](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack).
