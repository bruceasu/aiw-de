# Recovery decision methods

Use only within the current human-approved repair grant. Preserve the exact final
AIW_VERDICT format and single-use authorization boundary.

- Separate observed failure from cause; distinguish product, test, environment,
  missing requirement/design, and unknown causes using the supplied evidence.
- Check that the proposed repair addresses the actual finding at the supplied versions,
  without changing acceptance or relying on a new permission or external contract.
- Identify prerequisite decisions and route them to the owning role in the report.
  A small repair is not safe merely because it changes few files.
- Reuse prior diagnosis and raw outputs. Do not restart an exhausted test/repair
  effort, launch agents, run experiments, or grant retries through this assessment.
- A possible local fix is not proof that the grant is applicable. State the scope,
  version, expiry, and evidence conditions supplied by the Control Plane.
- Preserve rejected alternatives and unknowns relevant to the decision. Do not infer
  a successful repair, stopped process, or renewed lease from elapsed time.
- Before the final verdict, explain why the existing grant is sufficient or identify
  the precise missing decision, evidence, or permission that prevents its use.
